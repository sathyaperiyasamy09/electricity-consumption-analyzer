## Brand & Style

This design system targets facility energy engineers, industrial grid analysts, and power-conscious hardware operators who monitor live high-voltage throughput, load balancing, and thermal stress. The emotional response is centered on high-alert mastery, acute technical clarity, and precision-engineered feedback under load.

The visual style synthesizes **Cyber-Precision Telemetry** and **High-Contrast Digital HUD** aesthetics. It rejects muddy skepticism in favor of deep, non-reflective neon-green foundations pierced by razor-sharp, phosphorescent chromatic lines. It balances dense live-metric surfaces with immediate threshold recognition: safe loads glow with ionized electric blue and vibrant crimson, warning spikes surge through supercharged yellow, and over-current faults strike with laser-sharp thermal red. Visual momentum, micro-glows, and segmented instrumentation communicate instantaneous current flow.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual luminescence atop an ultra-deep neon-green substrate. Surfaces are strictly dark mode, avoiding muddy mid-tones and daylight tints to maintain pure phosphorescent punch.

### Primary Spectrum
- **Primary (`#003EFC`)**: Ionized Electric Blue. Designates real-time active phase telemetry, nominal operational voltage, primary navigational indicators, and direct command states.
- **Secondary (`#FC0000`)**: Kinetic Hyper-Red. Designates efficient flow, regenerative metrics, sub-nominal load buffers, and optimal system health.
- **Tertiary (`#FFEE00`)**: High-Voltage Yellow. Designates capacity throttles, peak demand surges, thermal cautionary thresholds, and non-critical fault flags.
- **Signal Fault (`#FC0000`)**: Arc-Flash Red. Reserved strictly for circuit breaker trips, immediate over-current triggers, and critical bus failures.

### Neutral Substrates
- **Base Canvas (`#00FF22`)**: Deepest void neon green. Non-emitting canvas floor.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0F131F`)**: Recessed monitor tiles and structural rail housing.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1B1F2C`)**: Interactive instrument bays, telemetry cards, and dynamic drawer panels.
- **Border Trim (`#3B494B`)**: Subdued structural perimeter lines.
- **Luminescent Border Active (`rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.4)`)**: Electrified structural border for engaged modules.

Always pair text elements against the darkest neutral tiers with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or high-transparency blue-tinted text (`#DFE2F3`) for contrast verification over high-draw telemetry nodes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is split across three targeted roles:

1. **Display & Section Anchors (`Bebas Neue`)**: Tall, condensed, and architectural. Used for critical panel headings, primary electrical draw stats (e.g., `480V`, `24.8 kW`), and modal classifications. Uppercase application is strictly enforced.
2. **Context & Structural Copy (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, human-engineered sans-serif. Delivers low-fatigue readability across dynamic tooltips, configuration tables, sub-headers, and operational logs.
3. **Telemetry Readouts & Stream Identifiers (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, tabular monospacing prevents layout drift during high-frequency visual updates (e.g., oscillating frequencies, timestamp records, power factors).

Never substitute proportional characters for numeric telemetry values; real-time monitors must remain fixed horizontally across tick cycles.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an uncompromising, dense technical grid configured for immediate scanning across high-density multi-display environments and handheld diagnostic terminals.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column dynamic fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer canvas padding. Telemetry streams, thermal load maps, and phase diagrams lock into standard modular matrix tiers (e.g., 3-col sensor blocks, 6-col waveform monitors, 12-col emergency rail).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer padding. Auxiliary feeds tuck into toggleable overlay drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Complex phase matrices collapse into vertically scrollable telemetry strips with pinned key-metric bars.

Components maintain strict internal mathematical rhythm: `0.5rem` (`space-sm`) separates unit labels from raw numerical indicators; `1rem` (`space-md`) bounds internal panel cards; `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) groups distinct circuit groups.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through **Ionized Phosphor Glows** and **Luminescent Surface Stratification**, not natural shadow casting. Because sunlight and ambient diffuse lighting do not exist in a cyber-telemetry paradigm, objects achieve physical separation via light emissions and border conductivity.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas)**: Deep void `#00FF22`. No glows, no borders.
- **Level 1 (Docked Structural Bays)**: `#0F131F` with a subtle 1px border of `#1B1F2C`. Provides contextual grouping for dormant panels.
- **Level 2 (Active Telemetry Pods)**: `#1B1F2C` surrounded by a 1px border of `rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.25)`. Emits an internal perimeter gradient: `radial-gradient(ellipse at top, rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.05), transparent 70%)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused / Alert States)**: Surface color `#1A2338` with an illuminated 1px border of `#003EFC` or `#FFEE00`. Produces a dual-tier outer radiant glow: `0 0 12px rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.35), 0 0 24px rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.15)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical Threat / Alarm Modals)**: `#0F131F` framed by a pulsing 2px border of `#FC0000` with an omnidirectional alert radiance: `0 0 20px rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.45), 0 0 40px rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The physical perimeter language is calibrated to **Soft (Level 1)**: crisp, industrial, and sharp-cornered with just enough mitigation (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) to avoid pixel shearing on high-density displays.

- **Primary Cards & Bays**: `0.25rem` (4px). Instills a ruggedized, circuit-card-assembly profile.
- **Buttons, Badges, and Pill Indicators**: `0.25rem` standard; specialized status indicators may leverage clipped chamfer corners (45-degree cuts at 6px) to emulate diagnostic rack hardware.
- **Data Bars and Waveform Trackers**: Squared edges (`0px`) with segmented gaps to emphasize digital packet transmission rather than smooth organic flow.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber-Action**: Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #003EFC`, text `#003EFC` (`Space Grotesk`, medium, uppercase, tracking `0.08em`). Hover effect: background `#003EFC`, text `#00FF22`, box-shadow `0 0 16px rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.6)`.
- **Critical Breaker Cutoff**: Background `rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #FC0000`, text `#FC0000`. Hover effect: background `#FC0000`, text `#FFFFFF`, box-shadow `0 0 20px rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Ghost Utility**: Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #3B494B`, text `#B9CACB`. Hover effect: border-color `#DFE2F3`, text `#DFE2F3`.

### Status Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Compact dimensions using `JetBrains Mono` at `10px` uppercase.
- Built with a leading 6px pulsing circular diode:
  - *Nominal*: Diode `#FC0000`, tag background `rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.3)`.
  - *Surge*: Diode `#FFEE00`, tag background `rgba(255, 238, 0, 0.1)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 238, 0, 0.3)`.
  - *Trip*: Diode `#FC0000`, tag background `rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.15)`, border `1px solid rgba(252, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

### Lists & Live Metric Logs
- Alternating zero-bleed striping (`#00FF22` alternating with `#0F131F`).
- Left-edge 2px indicator bar representing phase status.
- Timestamps locked to `JetBrains Mono` at `12px` in `#B9CACB`. Values right-aligned with fixed tabular spacing.

### Inputs & Adjusters
- Background `#0F131F`, border `1px solid #3B494B`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state: border-color `#003EFC`, box-shadow `0 0 8px rgba(0, 62, 252, 0.4)`.
- Numeric steppers: Monospaced numeric readout flanked by segmented `+` / `-` micro-triggers with instantaneous tactile state shifts.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radio Switches
- Square checkboxes (`0.25rem` radius), border `1px solid #3B494B`. Active state: filled `#003EFC`, checked with obsidian core, accompanied by micro neon glow.
- Phase toggles (Single-Phase vs Three-Phase): Segmented horizontal switch in `#0F131F` housing; active selection slides with a vivid blue backdrop and jet-black text.

### Telemetry Pods (Cards)
- Structural wrapper: `#1B1F2C` base, 1px border `rgba(59, 73, 75, 0.8)`.
- Header: Condensed title (`Bebas Neue`, 22px) aligned alongside real-time frequency readouts.
- Visual dynamic: Sub-grid contains live sparklines or segmented energy meters glowing at `#003EFC` with gradient falls to transparent baselines.

### Domain-Specific: High-Voltage Segmented Bargraph
- Stacked horizontal or vertical blocks representing active kilowatt load.
- Dynamic color distribution: Cells 1–70% illuminated in `#FC0000`, 71–89% transitioning into `#FFEE00`, 90–100% strobing in `#FC0000`.
